# Ram Kabir Sampraday — Research Brief (2026-08-21, Sonnet web-research agent)

**Source caveat:** almost no independent scholarship exists; nearly everything below is from the community's own sites (wp.ramkabir.org, ramkabirbhajans.org, ramkabirpanth.blogspot.com). Treat dates/lineage as community tradition.

## History
- Kabir (trad. c. 1440s–1518), nirgun bhakti, disciple of Ramanand — basis of "Ram-Kabir" synthesis. Kabir linked to Kabirvad (banyan island in the Narmada near Bharuch).
- "Uda Bhakt" from "Juda (different) Bhagat Patidar" — Patidar families who broke from mainstream Patel ritual practice; concentrated in South Gujarat (Surat), diaspora via Africa/Fiji to USA/UK.
- 1470–71: Padmanabhji + Dhanraj Pandya ("Adhyaruji Maharaj", of Patan) establish Padam Wadi, Patan. Adhyaruji wrote ~30 kirtans.
- Sadguru Jivanji Maharaj (1649–1737 per community): met Guru Gopaldas 1683, founded the "Uda Parampara" on Sharad Purnima 1688 at Shahpura, Vadodara dist. Associated with Puniyad village (Dabhoi taluka) — the Foundation's address on Sant Darshan's title page matches Puniyad.
- Sadguru Shree Jivanji Maharaj Memorial Foundation: founded 1994 by Premjivan Swami; the community's publishing arm (Google Play/Kindle ebooks).

## Texts
- **Adhyaruji na Kirtan / Parayan**: Adhyaruji's kirtans + Jivanji Maharaj's sakhis, combined for daily parayan recitation. (~180-yr gap between the two authors — later editorial compilation.)
- **Samagam** (Purvardh + Uttarardh): devotional anthology by Shree Haridas Nadiyada.
- **Sant Darshan**: saint-tradition literature (thin sourcing; publisher = the Foundation, Puniyad).
- Structure confirmed from our own English PDF: numbered **Ras** compositions (e.g. "Garuva Ganpatino Ras") with "Hari Hari ॥N॥" refrains.

## Community today
- Shree Ramkabir Bhakta Samaj of USA (roots 1973, renamed 1980; Bhakta Cultural Center, Norwalk CA 2002; ~12,000+ members).
- Shree Ramkabir Mandir, Carson CA (inaugurated 1994, Kabir Jayanti). NJ mandir NOT confirmed.
- ramkabirbhajans.org — big existing digital archive: ~300 audio, 1,000+ videos, Nad Brahma (900+ bhajans), scanned handwritten books to 1937. Worth linking from the app rather than duplicating audio.
- SRBS Houston (srbsofhouston.org), South East Uda Bhakt Samaj (seubs.org — SSL expired at check time).

## Glossary
kirtan (longer hymn) · sakhi/doha (couplet teaching-verse) · ras (numbered composition-form within the kirtan books) · parayan (daily recitation anthology/practice) · gutka (pocket recitation book) · Occhav kirtan (Sharad Purnima observance) · Uda Bhakt/Uda Vaishnav/Uda Patidar (community self-name).
